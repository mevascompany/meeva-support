Privacy Policy

Last updated: 02/10/2026

Welcome to the Privacy Policy of Meva Mobile Studio's mobile application ("Meeva", "the App", or "the Service"). Meeva helps users make decisions together by creating rooms and matching preferences for movies, games, books, places, and other activities.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how it is protected when you use Meeva.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account and Authentication Information

Meeva currently supports guest access using Firebase Authentication. When you use guest mode, Firebase creates a unique user identifier (Firebase User ID) for your session.

We do not require you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or physical address to use guest mode.

1.2 Room and Preference Data

To provide the room-based matching functionality, Meeva may store information associated with a room, including:

  • Room code
  • Host user ID
  • Guest user ID
  • Room status
  • Room creation time
  • User selections and matching information

This information is used to synchronize participants and provide the matching experience.

1.3 Movie and Content Data

Meeva uses The Movie Database (TMDB) API to retrieve movie and television information, such as titles, release dates, posters, genres, ratings, and descriptions.

TMDB content is provided through TMDB's services and is not maintained as a separate personal profile about you by Meeva.

1.4 Location Information

Some Meeva features, such as Places, may use approximate location information to help users discover nearby places or activities.

Meeva does not use precise location for advertising or behavioral tracking. Location access is used only when a feature requires it and according to the permissions granted by the user.

1.5 Technical Information

Depending on the services enabled in a particular version of the App, limited technical or diagnostic information may be processed by third-party infrastructure providers to operate and maintain the Service.

We do not use the App for targeted advertising, and Meeva does not contain third-party advertising networks.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected or processed through Meeva may be used for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate users and provide guest access
  • To create and manage rooms
  • To synchronize room participants in real time
  • To process preferences and matching results
  • To provide movie and other content discovery features
  • To provide location-based features when enabled
  • To maintain and improve app functionality
  • To diagnose technical problems and maintain security
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

3. Third-Party Services

Meeva uses certain third-party services to provide its functionality. These services may process information as necessary to provide their respective services.

3.1 Firebase

Meeva uses Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, for authentication, room management, and real-time synchronization.

Firebase may process information such as a unique user identifier and information stored in the room database in accordance with Google's Firebase privacy practices.

For more information, please review Google's privacy documentation: Google Privacy Policy

3.2 TMDB

Meeva uses TMDB to retrieve movie and television metadata. TMDB is a third-party content provider and its own terms and privacy practices apply to its services.

For more information: TMDB Privacy Policy

4. Advertising and Tracking

Meeva is designed to be ad-free.

We do not use third-party advertising networks to display advertisements in the App, and we do not use collected information for targeted advertising or cross-app behavioral tracking.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers such as Firebase and TMDB when necessary to provide the functionality described in this Privacy Policy.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through the Service against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Information stored in Firebase may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide room, matching, authentication, security, and operational functionality, or as otherwise required by applicable law.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it is processed.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, or otherwise control certain information.

You may also control optional permissions, such as location access, through your device settings.

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact us using the information below.

9. Children's Privacy

Meeva is a general-audience application and is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us in a manner that violates applicable law, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

10. International Data Processing

Because Meeva uses third-party services such as Firebase and TMDB, information processed through the Service may be stored or processed in countries outside your country of residence.

Where required, appropriate safeguards are used in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App, our services, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly.
